## Launch checklist

1. Create the YouTube channel and Instagram account with the names above (check the handles first).
2. Upload the profile picture to both, and the banner to YouTube.
3. Set "not made for kids", category Education, keywords and the watermark.
4. Post EP001 on both platforms at the same time of day you plan to post every day.
5. Pin the comment and reply to early comments (the replies feed future topics).
6. After 10–14 days, check retention graphs: where people swipe away tells us how to tune the hooks.
