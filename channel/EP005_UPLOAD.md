## YouTube Shorts

**Title**
What If You Had No Head? 😳 #shorts

**Description**
A viewer asked: what if we didn't have a head? 🤔

Problem 1: your ears are in your head. So you'd hear… nothing.
Problem 2: your voice doesn't even start in your head. It starts in your NECK, where two tiny vocal folds buzz over a hundred times a second. Your throat, mouth and nose shape that buzz into words.
No head? You'd be a kazoo that nobody can hear. Including you.

🔊 The buzz in this video is Pip's real voice with the "head" digitally removed (inverse filtering, the method voice scientists use to isolate the vocal folds).

Sources
• Fant, G. (1960). Acoustic Theory of Speech Production. (the source-filter model of the voice)
• Titze, I. R. (1994). Principles of Voice Production.
• University of Minnesota Lions Voice Clinic: How the voice works (average speaking pitch ≈115 Hz men, ≈200 Hz women)
